THE STORY / 02

Different background.
Same curiosity.

“What does the evidence actually say—and what should we do next?”

I studied chemistry at East Carolina University and worked in scientific and laboratory settings. That taught me to notice details, question assumptions, and stay curious when the first answer didn't hold up.

Over time, I wanted to apply that same thinking to a different problem: decisions that affect people and businesses. That's what brought me to Wake Forest's Master of Science in Business Analytics program.

Now I'm learning Python, SQL, machine learning, and decision modeling while working on real client problems. The technical work matters to me, but so does explaining the result in a way people can use.
