01 / FIRST-PLACE MODEL EVALUATION

Same Heart. Different Device.

ECG AI Build-A-Thon · October 2026 · Team project

1st place overall0.97 official combined AUC0.9895 local cross-device holdout AUROC

Question

Can two short Lead I ECG recordings from different devices be ranked by whether they come from the same person?

Approach

Our team used three Siamese 1D ResNet encoders and averaged similarity scores. Patient-disjoint evaluation helped protect against leakage.

My role

I contributed independent evaluation, checkpoint auditing, ensemble-weight and recording-length experiments, reproducibility checks, and documentation. Other teammates led model training and data ingestion.

Limits and interpretation

The 0.97 score is the official competition result; 0.9895 is a different local clinical-to-Apple-Watch holdout result. This is not a medical diagnostic or verified identity system.

03 / ADVANCED FRAUD MODELING

Fraud models for real decisions.

Wake Forest MSBA Analytics Challenge · Team project · 2026

190,517 applications35 predictors0.872 XGBoost ROC AUC

Question

How can application signals help identify risk without creating an unmanageable review burden or an unfair experience for legitimate customers?

Approach

Explored signals including application velocity, device behavior, identity stability, and linked entities, with tree-based risk modeling and an operating-point analysis.

My role

Participated in the team’s modeling, evaluation, and business recommendation work connecting predictions to fraud costs, false positives, and review capacity.

Limits and interpretation

A course simulation, not a production fraud model. At the evaluated scenario, 62.7% of fraud was captured at 36.5% precision; results depend on the selected threshold.
