Build real conversations that lead to a better understanding of 2027 entry-level business analyst, strategy & analytics, analytics consulting, product/business operations, and rotational roles. Prioritize fit and personal relevance over mass-messaging.

Check that every job is genuinely open, eligible for May 2027 graduates, and aligned with your preferred skills before requesting a referral.

## Weekly outreach workflow (suggested)
1. Pick 3–5 live, best-fit openings. Save links, role requirements, location and application deadline.
2. Find 4–6 relevant people through existing relationships, alumni connections and public employee profiles.
3. Send short, individualized outreach grounded in a real common interest or role; avoid asking for a referral in the first sentence.
4. Follow up once after about 7 business days if there was no reply. Stop after the follow-up unless the contact invites more.
5. After a genuine conversation, ask whether the role is a good match and whether they would feel comfortable referring you.
6. Keep a private contact tracker (not committed to this public repo).

Do not commit personal contact information, private messages, referral codes, or application credentials to a public repository.

## Message templates

### Alumni / new connection
Hi [First name]! I’m a Wake Forest MSBA student graduating next May, and I came across your work in [team/role]. I’m exploring analytics and strategy roles and would love to hear a little about your experience at [company]. If you have 15 minutes sometime, I’d really appreciate it.

### After a useful chat
Thanks again for taking the time to talk. Your point about [specific insight] was really helpful. I found a [role title] opening that seems aligned with what we discussed. Would you be comfortable taking a quick look and letting me know if it sounds like the right fit? No worries either way.

### Follow-up after no response
Hi [First name], just following up on my note from last week. I know things get busy, so no pressure at all. I’d still appreciate any perspective you have on [specific role/team]. Thanks!

### After a referral
I really appreciate you putting in a referral for me. I’ve submitted the application and will keep you posted if I hear anything. Thanks again for helping me out!
