# Measuring Partnership Value

**Allegacy Credit Union × Wake Forest · Academic client project · Fall 2026**

## Question
How can the institution evaluate the impact of its university partnership beyond counts of activities and events?

## My contribution
- Synthesized client discovery discussions on engagement, member relationships, outreach and the partnership's intended value.
- Proposed a scorecard spanning engagement, relationship growth, financial well-being and community impact.
- Helped frame stakeholder-facing alternatives and suggested testing targeted invitations and post-event follow-up.

## Status and limits
The work represents **discovery, measurement design and recommendations**. Proposed measures and tests have **not** been shown to cause membership growth or other realized financial results.
