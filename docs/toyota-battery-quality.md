# Battery Winding Quality Drivers

**Toyota Battery Manufacturing North Carolina · Wake Forest MSBA client practicum · October 2026–present**

## Client question
How might battery winding process settings, measurements, and inspections interact with downstream quality and defect risk?

## My role — Student Client Liaison
- Coordinate questions, discussions and follow-up between the student team and client.
- Translate a broad manufacturing challenge into more specific business decisions, outcome definitions, data availability and traceability requirements.
- Help identify success criteria, evaluation trade-offs and dependencies for future quality analysis.
- Contributed to team knowledge-management and modeling-question workflows.

## Status and limits
The project is in **client discovery and analytical scoping**, not completed model deployment. There are **no verified claims** of improved yield, reduced defects, or implemented production controls. Proprietary plant information is deliberately excluded.

This is an **academic capstone client project, not employment at Toyota**.
